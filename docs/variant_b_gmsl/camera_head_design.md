## 2.1 Power tree

```
coax centre ─PoC L (2 stages)─► VPOC 9–16 V ─TVS─┬─ TPS62160 buck ─► +3V6 (VSYS, 1 A)
                                                  │
+3V6 ─┬─ TLV62569 buck ──► +1V1_BUCK ─ TPS22918 (ON=EN_1V1, CT) ─► +1V1 ─┬─ 10 µH ─► +1V1_LIF  (VDDLIF)
      │                                                                   └─ 10 µH ─► +1V1_PLA  (VDDLPLA)
      ├─ TLV75518 LDO ──► +1V8 (always on: MAX9295A VDD18/VDDIO)
      │                    └ TPS22918 (ON=EN_1V8S) ─► +1V8_SNS (VDDMIO, OSC, pull-ups)
      ├─ LP5907-2.9 ─────► TPS22918 (ON=EN_2V9) ─► +2V9  (VDDH*)
      └─ LP5907-3.3 ─────► TPS22918 (ON=EN_3V3) ─► +3V3A (VDD33*)
```

* **Sequencing (DS 17.1):** DVDD 1.1 → OVDD 1.8 → AVDD2 2.9 → AVDD1 3.3, all within 200 ms. Power-off runs in reverse (DS 17.3). The four `EN_*` lines come from MAX9295A MFPs, and `nv_imx568.c` (`imx568_rails()`) toggles them in order with 2 ms steps. 100 kΩ pull-downs keep every sensor rail off while the serializer boots.
* **Slew ≤ 25 mV/µs (DS 17.2):** the LDOs start too fast (LP5907 ≈ 80 µs), so each rail goes through a TPS22918 with a CT capacitor. At 3.3 V the ramp must be ≥ 132 µs; target 0.5–1 ms and pick CT from the TPS22918 rise-time table. TPS22918 QOD (quick output discharge) gives a clean, ordered power-down.
* **Current (DS 11.2):** typ / max = 3.3 V 28/45 mA, 2.9 V 79/125 mA, 1.8 V 0.1/2 mA, 1.1 V 153/265 mA. The sensor draws ≈ 0.5 W typ and 0.86 W worst case. Budget about 2 W per head including the serializer (VERIFY MAX9295A current) and conversion losses.
* MAX9295A core supply: use the internal regulator or an external rail exactly as the MAX9295A datasheet specifies (VERIFY).

**INCK gating:** the oscillator's OE follows XCLR through 10 kΩ / 10 nF (≈100 µs), with a BAT54 for fast turn-off. INCK therefore starts ≥ 1 µs after XCLR rises (DS 17.1 T5) and is Hi-Z while the sensor is in reset or off.

Avoid MFPs that have strap or alternate functions (CFG, LOCK, ERRB, RCLKOUT, I²C pass-through) for the six GPIO roles. Check the MAX9295A pin table.

## 2.4 Coax / PoC

* Connector: 50 Ω vertical board-mount (FAKRA-Z or mini-FAKRA/HFM for compact heads).
* Size the PoC filter (typically two inductor stages + ferrite) with ADI's GMSL PoC calculator for the cable length, and check the insertion-loss/return-loss mask. 12 V nominal, 9–16 V accepted by the TPS62160 front end. TVS on VPOC.

## 2.5 Mechanical

* Board 30 × 30 mm, sensor centred, so the optical axis is the board centre. The package centre is the optical centre within ±0.075 mm (DS 7).
* M12 lens holder rated for **1/1.8″ (8.8 mm image circle)**. Holes at ±11 mm in the demo; adapt to the holder used. 4 × M2 mounting holes on a 25 mm square.
* Stereo pair: mount both heads on one rigid bracket and keep the baseline fixed. Same lens model and focus on both.
* Mono sensor with response up to ~1000 nm (DS 13): use an IR-cut filter only if needed. DS 21.5(5) warns about NIR leakage into the optical-black area with some IR-cut filters.
